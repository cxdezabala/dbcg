About this prototype
SYNERGON — interactive MVP

One platform: a shared Intelligence Core, five executive components and the SYNERGON Command leadership layer. Use the Demo role switcher and the Scenario menu to walk the end-to-end story.

Implemented (P0 / P1)
Role-based navigation and data scope · shared state across modules · CRM-style account intelligence · Radar360 with verified vs. hypothesis labelling · seven-stage Solution Design Studio with live business case · expert finder, requests and knowledge validation · contextual learning, quiz and role-play · leadership command center with collective intelligence, heatmap with privacy thresholds, initiatives · notifications · audit log · light/dark modes · local persistence.
Simulated
All AI responses are deterministic and labelled. All companies, people and sources are fictional. Role switching is a demo device — production authorisation is enforced server-side.
Planned (P2)
Live CRM and market feeds · enterprise SSO · production AI orchestration · real-time collaboration · learning-platform integration · document export · knowledge graph · forecasting · security certifications.
Design note
Visual identity follows the project’s attached Modernist design system (ink, single accent, zero radius, strong rules) rather than the brief’s navy/teal palette.
